I'm a first-semester **BS Computer Science** student at **GUTECH**. Before university I represented **Pakistan twice at the FIRST Global Challenge**, led a **50+ team robotics competition** as Vice President of a robotics society, and appeared on national TV and podcasts to encourage students into STEM.

Now I'm turning that hardware, leadership and communication experience into software, building real tools for real problems.
